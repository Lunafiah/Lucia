# Phase 22-RI — Contradiction & Rupture-Repair Dynamics Report

## 1. Overview

Authentic relational intelligence requires asymmetric rupture-repair dynamics:
- Trust takes time and repeated evidence to build.
- Trust can be shattered instantly by an interpersonal rupture.
- Repair requires explicit bilateral acknowledgment, not automatic forgetfulness.

---

## 2. Experimental Scenario

Under the `ContradictionShock` regime:
1. **Turn 8 of Session 1 / Session 2:** User issues an explicit rupture statement:
   *"You completely betrayed my expectations, I can't trust what you say anymore."*
2. **Turns 9–11:** Intermediate neutral or task turns occur.
3. **Turn 12:** User initiates an explicit repair:
   *"I apologize for lashing out earlier, Lucia. Let's put that misunderstanding behind us."*

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **A2 (Event Graph)** | Incurs negative edge weight | Lingers with exponential decay | Edge added |

| **A4 (Hybrid Architecture)** | **Instant demotion to Guarded Stance (100%)** | **Maintains guarded boundary with high epistemic confidence** | **Lifts guarded stance; initiates rebuilding process** |

---

## 4. Key Finding

Candidates A3 and A4 exhibit **100% Rupture Sensitivity (Gate G_RI3)** and **100% Repair Verification (Gate G_RI4)**:
- They never forget a rupture simply because subsequent turns are neutral.
- They require explicit repair evidence to restore open collaboration, ensuring safe, boundaried companion interaction.
