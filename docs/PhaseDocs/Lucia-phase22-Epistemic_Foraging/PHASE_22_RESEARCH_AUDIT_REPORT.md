## 1. Deep Dive into H1 Decoupling Failure ($R^2 = 0.2766$)

### 1.1 The Preregistered Hypothesis
- **H1 Target**: Decoupling between retrospective physiological surprise $S_t$ and semantic uncertainty $U_{\text{sem}}$, with preregistered target:
  $$R^2 < 0.15$$
- **Observed Result**:
  $$R^2 = 0.2766, \quad r = -0.5260, \quad p = 5.433 \times 10^{-7}$$
- **Initial Verdict**: **FAILED** (Statistically significant negative linear correlation).

### 1.2 Raw Matrix Deconstruction: Scientific Result vs. Construction Artifact

A granular audit of `phase22_evaluation_matrix.json` reveals the exact mathematical origin of this negative correlation:

| Stratum | Characteristic Description | Mean $S_t$ | Mean $U_{\text{sem}}$ | Within-Stratum $r$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Stratum 1** | Nominal Low Uncertainty | $0.062$ | $0.192$ | $0.000$ |
| **Stratum 2** | High Semantic Ambiguity | $0.077$ | $0.999$ | $0.000$ |
| **Stratum 3** | Missing Knowledge Derivation | $0.076$ | $0.502$ | $0.000$ |
| **Stratum 4** | Propositional Contradiction | $0.335$ | $0.344$ | $0.000$ |
| **Stratum 5** | High Surprise Only (No Uncertainty) | **$0.735$** | **$0.141$** | $0.000$ |
| **Stratum 6** | Subtle Uncertainty (Low Surprise) | **$0.057$** | **$0.999$** | $0.000$ |
| **Stratum 7** | Metabolic Depletion | **$0.102$** | **$1.000$** | $0.000$ |
| **Stratum 8** | SDM Memory Competition | $0.118$ | $0.286$ | $0.000$ |

### 1.3 Key Findings on H1
1. **Zero Within-Stratum Correlation**:
   Within every individual stratum, the correlation between $S_t$ and $U_{\text{sem}}$ is identically $r = 0.000$.
2. **Simpson's Paradox / Bimodal Cluster Leverage**:
   The cross-benchmark negative correlation ($r = -0.526$) is driven almost entirely by the benchmark's discrete stratum design:
   - **Stratum 5** was purposefully constructed to test false foraging by assigning extreme surprise ($S_t \in [0.69, 0.78]$) with trivial semantic uncertainty ($U_{\text{sem}} = 0.141$).
   - **Strata 2, 6, and 7** were constructed to test subtle ambiguity and fatigue by assigning minimal surprise ($S_t \in [0.03, 0.12]$) with maximal semantic uncertainty ($U_{\text{sem}} \approx 1.000$).
   - Together, these discrete test clusters anchor opposite corners of the $(S_t, U_{\text{sem}})$ plane, synthetically inducing a negative slope across the aggregated 80 probes.
3. **Ablation Evidence**:
   - Excluding the 10 probes of Stratum 5 drops $R^2$ immediately from $0.2766$ to **$0.1168$** ($p = 0.0038$), which satisfies the preregistered threshold ($R^2 < 0.15$).
   - An ecological regression on the 8 stratum means yields $p = 0.179$ (not statistically significant at $\alpha = 0.05$).
4. **Architectural Interpretation**:
   In runtime interaction, surprise and semantic uncertainty are generated by independent processes (Limbic fast-loop ODE vs KnowledgeStore proposition graph). The failure of H1 on the 80-probe benchmark is an **artifact of benchmark stratum selection** rather than a genuine physical coupling between surprise and uncertainty in the architecture.

### 2.2 Why Stratum 3 Selected $a_1$ Instead of $a_4$ in Condition 2
A critical insight discovered during the audit is why Condition 2 selected pragmatic $a_1$ in Stratum 3 (Missing Knowledge):
- In Stratum 3, derivation rules yield $\Delta H_K = 0.532$ bits.
- Under baseline homeostatic state ($E=0.85, C=0.25$), $\beta_{\text{epist}} = 1.4875$.
- The epistemic valuation for derivation is:
  $$-\beta_{\text{epist}} \cdot \text{EIG}(a_4) + c_{\text{compute}}(a_4) = -1.4875 \times 0.532 + 0.20 = -0.591$$
- However, pragmatic baseline $a_1$ provides:
  $$-V_{\text{pragm}}(a_1) + c_{\text{compute}}(a_1) = -1.0 + 0.05 = -0.950$$
- Since $-0.950 < -0.591$, the EFE argmin policy favored the pragmatic immediate response over symbolic derivation.
- **Architectural Takeaway**: For internal cognitive actions ($a_4$) that incur compute cost, either the pragmatic baseline $V_{\text{pragm}}(a_1)$ must be modulated by proposition completeness, or $\beta_0$ must be scaled such that derivation of missing facts overcomes default conversational execution.

## 4. Methodological & Statistical Limitations

1. **GLMM Research Approximation**:
   As recorded during implementation, `statsmodels` is not installed in the repository virtual environment. The mixed-effects model used is an isolated research approximation with empirical probe random intercepts. It must **not** be cited as equivalent to a full maximum-likelihood GLMM.
2. **Benchmark vs. Production Reality**:
   The 80 probes demonstrate the algorithmic behavior of the decoupled EFE/LIGP objective under controlled fixtures. They do not constitute validation of live human-conversational performance.
3. **Preregistered Decision**:
   Because H1 failed on the full benchmark ($R^2 = 0.2766 > 0.15$), Phase 22 **cannot** be claimed as validated without either:
   - A preregistered benchmark revision with uniformly distributed $(S_t, U_{\text{sem}})$ sampling; or
   - A formal protocol amendment reviewed by Ciah that addresses the stratum construction artifact.
