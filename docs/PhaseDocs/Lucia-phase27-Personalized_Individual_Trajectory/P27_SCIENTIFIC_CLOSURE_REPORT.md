## 2. Research Question & Inquiry Scope

The formal research scope allocated to Phase 27 investigated:

> **Primary Research Question (P27):**  
> *Does long-horizon, history-dependent behavioral and relational differentiation participate in a coherent, irreversible, and personalized developmental trajectory unique to Lucia's single continuous relationship with the user over months and years, requiring new trajectory-level state variables, maturation dynamics, or cognitive subsystems beyond the established baseline?*

Historically, developmental personalization was conceptualized as a future milestone guarding against generic reset and explaining long-term identity coherence. The objective of the boundary audit was to evaluate whether this concept represents an actual unmodeled mathematical gap in Lucia's multi-timescale architecture or whether it is already accounted for by lower-level mechanisms.

## 7. Phase 26 Claim C3 Scope Limitation

A crucial finding of the boundary audit concerns the relationship between Phase 26 Claim C3 and Phase 27:

### The Mechanistic Reality of P26 C3
In Phase 26, Claim C3 (*History Endogeneity*) was audited with the verdict:
$$\mathbf{C3\_CAUSAL\_IDENTIFIABILITY = NOT\_ESTABLISHED}$$
The root cause was strictly localized to the experimental apparatus:
- The distinction between `ACTIVE` and `YOKED_REPLAY` was recorded exclusively in the external `ActionLedger` and trial bookkeeping.
- Action provenance was **never passed into the internal state tensors** of `ResearchReplica` (`EmotionCore`, `SDMCore`, `PromptAssembler`). Both active and yoked instances received identical internal state updates ($S_{K, \text{active}} \equiv S_{K, \text{yoked}}$).

### Strict Scope Limitation Invariant
> **MANDATORY GOVERNANCE INVARIANT:**  
> **Agents must NOT reinterpret P26 C3 as a universal identifiability failure across the entire cognitive architecture.**  
> - P26 C3 was an apparatus-specific defect of that particular test harness, caused by the omission of an internal provenance representation.  
> - It does **not** imply that all endogenous processes are unidentifiable, nor that causal identifiability fails system-wide.  
> - In P27, the identifiability boundary arises from a different, fundamental methodological issue: the impossibility of cleanly separating multi-year developmental maturation from user co-adaptation and stimulus distribution history in simulation.

### Why Higher-Order Hypotheses are `BLOCKED_BY_IDENTIFIABILITY`
1. **Confounding with User Co-Adaptation:** In any longitudinal companion relationship, the human user adapts their language, pacing, and conversational habits to the agent simultaneously with the agent's adaptation. Disentangling true autonomous developmental maturation from human-driven prompting drift is structurally unidentifiable without an unfeasible counterfactual human control twin.
2. **Confounding with Exposure Distribution History:** Long-term divergence across months is heavily dominated by the specific sequence of topics, emotional shocks, and conversational contexts encountered. Separating endogenous developmental maturation from input-stream path dependency requires replica ensembles that cannot exist for a unique live individual companion.
3. **Preservation of Hypotheses:** Because these higher-order phenomena are blocked by identifiability rather than falsified by evidence, **they must not be claimed to be disproven or nonexistent**. They are retained under the controlled status `BLOCKED_BY_IDENTIFIABILITY`.
