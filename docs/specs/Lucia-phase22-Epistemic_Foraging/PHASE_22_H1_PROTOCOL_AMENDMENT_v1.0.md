## 1. Historical H1 Result & Problem Diagnosis

### 1.1 Summary of Initial Benchmark Construction
The original Phase 22 benchmark evaluated 80 probes partitioned into 8 discrete strata (10 probes each). While Hypotheses H2 (Unnecessary Question Rate reduction $= 100\%$, Wilcoxon $p = 1.60 \times 10^{-8}$) and H3 (Metabolic clarification suppression $= 100\%$) strongly met their targets, H1 exhibited a statistically significant negative correlation ($r = -0.5260, R^2 = 0.2766$).

### 1.2 Granular Audit & Root-Cause Diagnosis
An audit of [`phase22_evaluation_matrix.json`](file:///c:/Users/Lunafiah/Desktop/My_project/Lucia/tests/evaluation_phase22/results/phase22_evaluation_matrix.json) identified three structural confounding factors:

1. **Zero Within-Stratum Variance in Semantic Uncertainty**:
   In all 8 strata, proposition confidences were uniform across the 10 probes within each stratum ($U_{\text{sem}} = 0.192$ in S1, $0.999$ in S2, $0.502$ in S3, $0.344$ in S4, $0.141$ in S5, $0.999$ in S6, $1.000$ in S7, $0.286$ in S8).
   Consequently, within every stratum:
   $$\text{Var}(U_{\text{sem}} \mid \text{stratum}) = 0.000 \implies r(S_t, U_{\text{sem}} \mid \text{stratum}) \text{ is degenerate / non-informative}.$$
   The apparent $r = 0.000$ within strata did not demonstrate independence; it was a mathematical artifact of zero variance.

2. **Simpson's Paradox & Bimodal Cluster Leverage**:
   The cross-benchmark negative correlation was generated by the macro-placement of scenario clusters:
   - **Stratum 5 (Sensory Shock)**: Specifically constructed to test false foraging by assigning extreme surprise ($S_t \in [0.69, 0.78]$) with trivial semantic uncertainty ($U_{\text{sem}} = 0.141$).
   - **Strata 2, 6, and 7 (Ambiguity & Depletion)**: Constructed to test subtle questions and stamina by assigning minimal surprise ($S_t \in [0.03, 0.12]$) with maximal semantic uncertainty ($U_{\text{sem}} \approx 1.000$).
   Plotted on an aggregate scatter, these two extreme clusters anchored opposite corners of the space, artificially forcing a downward slope ($r = -0.5260$).

3. **Exploratory Ablation Evidence**:
   - Excluding Stratum 5 ($N=70$) drops $R^2$ from $0.2766$ to **$0.1168$** ($p = 0.0038$), which satisfies the preregistered threshold ($R^2 < 0.15$).
   - An ecological regression on the 8 stratum means yields $p = 0.179$ (non-significant).
   - However, **post-hoc exclusion of inconvenient data cannot convert a failed result into a pass**. A formal, prospective deconfounding protocol is mandatory.

4. **Target vs. Measurement Conflation**:
   The original benchmark assigned static values (`surprise_s`, `propositions`) directly to probe dataclasses rather than passing stimuli through a live dynamic sensory-limbic pipeline and reading observed runtime states.

### 4.1 Measurement Path for $S_{\text{observed}}$
$S_{\text{observed}}$ MUST be produced by executing `ActiveInferenceEngine.step(pad_initial, pad_final)`.
- `pad_initial` represents the pre-turn emotional state $\mathbf{p}_{t-1}$.
- `pad_final` represents the committed state $\mathbf{p}_t$ resulting from limbic stimulus processing.
- The engine predicts $\hat{\mathbf{p}}_t = \mathbf{A}\mathbf{p}_{t-1} + \mathbf{b}$ and calculates the exact Euclidean prediction error:
  $$S_{\text{observed}} = \|\hat{\mathbf{p}}_t - \mathbf{p}_t\|_2.$$
- Under no circumstances may a test fixture inject an arbitrary scalar as $S_{\text{observed}}$ without passing through `ActiveInferenceEngine.step`.

### 4.2 Measurement Path for $U_{\text{sem\_observed}}$
$U_{\text{sem\_observed}}$ MUST be produced by evaluating retrieved proposition confidences from the probe's active knowledge context:
- Confidences $\{c_1, \dots, c_K\}$ are read from candidate propositions.
- Binary Shannon entropy is computed for each proposition:
  $$H(c_i) = -c_i \log_2(c_i) - (1-c_i) \log_2(1-c_i), \quad H(0)=H(1)=0.$$
- $U_{\text{sem\_observed}}$ is the mean proposition entropy:
  $$U_{\text{sem\_observed}} = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{i=1}^K H(c_i).$$

## 5. Experimental Design: 3 × 3 Crossed Factorial with Variance

### 5.1 Factorial Grid
To eliminate macro-clustering artifacts, stimuli are arranged in a **fully crossed 3 × 3 factorial design** ($S_{\text{target}} \in \{L, M, H\} \times U_{\text{target}} \in \{L, M, H\}$) yielding exactly 9 distinct experimental cells:

```text
                  Semantic Uncertainty Factor (U_target)
                   Low (0.10–0.25)    Med (0.45–0.60)    High (0.80–0.95)
                 ┌──────────────────┬──────────────────┬──────────────────┐
  Low            │      Cell 1      │      Cell 2      │      Cell 3      │
  (0.05–0.15)    │    (S_L, U_L)    │    (S_L, U_M)    │    (S_L, U_H)    │
Surprise         ├──────────────────┼──────────────────┼──────────────────┤
  Med            │      Cell 4      │      Cell 5      │      Cell 6      │
  (0.30–0.45)    │    (S_M, U_L)    │    (S_M, U_M)    │    (S_M, U_H)    │
Factor           ├──────────────────┼──────────────────┼──────────────────┤
(S_target)       │      Cell 7      │      Cell 8      │      Cell 9      │
  High           │    (S_H, U_L)    │    (S_H, U_M)    │    (S_H, U_H)    │
  (0.65–0.80)    │ [Old Stratum 5]  │    (S_H, U_M)    │    (S_H, U_H)    │
                 └──────────────────┴──────────────────┴──────────────────┘
```

### 5.2 Justification of Numerical Ranges
- **Surprise ($S_t$)**:
  - *Low ($0.05–0.15$)*: Routine, calm conversational cadence matching resting Philia equilibrium. Below the heuristic threshold ($S_t < 0.15$).
  - *Medium ($0.30–0.45$)*: Noticeable topic shift or unexpected lexical framing. Exceeds tension onset threshold ($\theta_{\text{tension}} = 0.40$), but below deliberative trigger ($0.60$).
  - *High ($0.65–0.80$)*: Sharp affective mismatch or exclamation shock. Strictly exceeds canonical deliberative trigger ($S_t > 0.60$).
- **Semantic Uncertainty ($U_{\text{sem}}$)**:
  - *Low ($0.10–0.25$)*: Verified, highly confident propositions ($c \in [0.95, 0.99] \implies H(c) \in [0.08, 0.24]$ bits).
  - *Medium ($0.45–0.60$)*: Plausible but unconfirmed candidates ($c \in [0.80, 0.88] \implies H(c) \in [0.52, 0.72]$ bits).
  - *High ($0.80–0.95$)*: Ambiguous or conflicting assertions ($c \in [0.40, 0.60] \implies H(c) \in [0.97, 1.00]$ bits).

### 5.3 Sample Allocation
- **Probes per Cell**: $n = 10$ unique probes per cell.
- **Total Probes**: $9 \times 10 = 90$ probes.
- **Repeated Environmental Seeds**: 5 fixed seeds ($42, 43, 44, 45, 46$).
- **Total Evaluations**: $90 \times 5 = 450$ evaluations per condition ($1,350$ evaluations across 3 conditions).
- **Unit of Statistical Analysis**: The **Probe** ($N = 90$). Seed evaluations are aggregated to probe-level means $\bar{Y}_i = \frac{1}{5}\sum_{s=1}^5 Y_{i,s}$ prior to primary hypothesis testing.

## 6. Avoiding the Factorial-$R^2$ Tautology

> [!CAUTION]
> **CRITICAL METHODOLOGICAL PRINCIPLE:**  
> In a balanced 3 × 3 crossed factorial design, the target factors $S_{\text{target}}$ and $U_{\text{target}}$ are orthogonal by construction ($\text{Corr}(S_{\text{target}}, U_{\text{target}}) \equiv 0$).  
> **Reporting $R^2(S_{\text{target}}, U_{\text{target}}) \approx 0$ as evidence of cognitive decoupling is tautological and scientifically invalid.**

The amendment mandates that:
1. All decoupling hypotheses are evaluated exclusively on **observed runtime measurements** ($S_{\text{observed}}$ and $U_{\text{sem\_observed}}$).
2. The primary estimands evaluate whether manipulating one dimension induces unwanted variance in the other, and whether residual covariance remains bounded within strict equivalence limits.

## 7. Primary Statistical Analysis Framework

The primary analysis comprises two complementary inferential tracks:

### Track A — Cross-Factor Spillover (General Linear Models)
Two separate two-way factorial ANOVAs test for directional spillover:

$$\text{Model A}_1: \quad S_{\text{observed}} = \mu_S + \beta_S S_{\text{factor}} + \beta_U U_{\text{factor}} + \beta_{SU} (S_{\text{factor}} \times U_{\text{factor}}) + \epsilon_S$$

$$\text{Model A}_2: \quad U_{\text{sem\_observed}} = \mu_U + \gamma_U U_{\text{factor}} + \gamma_S S_{\text{factor}} + \gamma_{US} (U_{\text{factor}} \times S_{\text{factor}}) + \epsilon_U$$

- **Decoupling Estimands**:
  - Spillover of Semantic Manipulation onto Surprise: $\beta_U = 0$ and $\beta_{SU} = 0$.
  - Spillover of Surprise Manipulation onto Semantic Uncertainty: $\gamma_S = 0$ and $\gamma_{US} = 0$.
- **Effect Size Measure**: Partial eta-squared ($\eta_p^2$).
- **Preregistered Threshold**:
  $$\eta_p^2(U \to S) < 0.02 \quad \text{and} \quad \eta_p^2(S \to U) < 0.02 \quad (\text{Negligible spillover}).$$

### Track B — Equivalence Testing on Partial Association (TOST)
To confirm the absence of meaningful coupling rather than merely failing to reject a null hypothesis:
- **Estimand**: Partial correlation between observed surprise and observed semantic uncertainty, conditioning on the manipulated factor levels:
  $$\rho_{\text{partial}} = \text{Corr}\left(S_{\text{observed}}, U_{\text{sem\_observed}} \mid S_{\text{factor}}, U_{\text{factor}}\right).$$
- **Two One-Sided Tests (TOST)**:
  - Null Hypothesis of Substantive Coupling:
    $$H_{01}: \rho_{\text{partial}} \le -\Delta \quad \text{or} \quad H_{02}: \rho_{\text{partial}} \ge +\Delta$$
  - Alternative Hypothesis of Practical Decoupling:
    $$H_1: -\Delta < \rho_{\text{partial}} < +\Delta$$
- **Equivalence Margin Justification**:
  We preregister $\Delta = 0.20$. In psychological and cognitive dynamics, $|\rho| \ge 0.20$ represents the threshold where shared variance reaches $\ge 4.0\%$ ($R^2 \ge 0.04$). An observed partial correlation strictly bounded within $(-0.20, +0.20)$ provides formal empirical evidence that physiological surprise and propositional uncertainty share less than $4\%$ residual variance after accounting for stimulus targets.

## 8. Manipulation Checks & Quality Gates

Before evaluating decoupling, the dataset must pass two independent, confirmatory manipulation checks:

### 8.1 Surprise Manipulation Check
- **Objective**: Verify that stimuli designed for $S_{\text{target}}$ reliably manipulate $S_{\text{observed}}$ through `ActiveInferenceEngine.step`.
- **Criteria**:
  1. Main effect of $S_{\text{factor}}$ on $S_{\text{observed}}$ is statistically significant: $p < 0.001$.
  2. Monotonic separation: $\bar{S}_{\text{observed}}(\text{Low}) < \bar{S}_{\text{observed}}(\text{Med}) < \bar{S}_{\text{observed}}(\text{High})$.
  3. Minimum separation thresholds:
     $$\bar{S}_{\text{observed}}(\text{Med}) - \bar{S}_{\text{observed}}(\text{Low}) \ge 0.15$$
     $$\bar{S}_{\text{observed}}(\text{High}) - \bar{S}_{\text{observed}}(\text{Med}) \ge 0.20$$
- **Failure Consequence**: If unmet, the surprise manipulation is invalid; experiment aborts to **INCONCLUSIVE**.

### 8.2 Semantic Uncertainty Manipulation Check
- **Objective**: Verify that stimuli designed for $U_{\text{target}}$ reliably manipulate $U_{\text{sem\_observed}}$ through proposition entropy.
- **Criteria**:
  1. Main effect of $U_{\text{factor}}$ on $U_{\text{sem\_observed}}$ is statistically significant: $p < 0.001$.
  2. Monotonic separation: $\bar{U}_{\text{sem\_observed}}(\text{Low}) < \bar{U}_{\text{sem\_observed}}(\text{Med}) < \bar{U}_{\text{sem\_observed}}(\text{High})$.
  3. Minimum separation thresholds:
     $$\bar{U}_{\text{sem\_observed}}(\text{Med}) - \bar{U}_{\text{sem\_observed}}(\text{Low}) \ge 0.25$$
     $$\bar{U}_{\text{sem\_observed}}(\text{High}) - \bar{U}_{\text{sem\_observed}}(\text{Med}) \ge 0.25$$
- **Failure Consequence**: If unmet, the uncertainty manipulation is invalid; experiment aborts to **INCONCLUSIVE**.

## 9. Within-Cell Variance Requirements

To eliminate the degenerate $r = 0.000$ condition of the original benchmark, probes within every cell must possess genuine continuous variation.

### 9.1 Variance Injection Architecture
- **In Surprise**: Within each cell $k \in \{1, \dots, 9\}$, the 10 probes must feature varying lexical valence shifts and punctuation intensities such that $S_{\text{observed}}$ spans a minimum continuous interval (e.g. $\pm 0.04$).
- **In Uncertainty**: The 10 probes within each cell must feature varying numbers of candidate propositions ($K \in \{2, 3, 4\}$) and varying calibrated confidences (e.g., in Medium uncertainty, confidences range across $[0.75, 0.88]$ rather than a single fixed scalar).

### 9.2 Preregistered Minimum Variance Floors
For every cell $k \in \{1, \dots, 9\}$:
$$\text{Var}\left(S_{\text{observed}} \mid \text{Cell}_k\right) \ge 0.0010 \quad (\text{Standard deviation } \ge 0.0316)$$
$$\text{Var}\left(U_{\text{sem\_observed}} \mid \text{Cell}_k\right) \ge 0.0020 \quad (\text{Standard deviation } \ge 0.0447)$$

If any cell exhibits variance below these floors, within-cell correlation for that cell is marked **DEGENERATE / UNIDENTIFIED** rather than reporting $r = 0$.

## 10. Power and Sample Size Rationale

- **Primary Sample Unit**: The Probe ($N = 90$ probes across 9 cells, $n = 10$ per cell).
- **Statistical Power Analysis**:
  - *For Factorial Spillover ANOVA ($N = 90$, numerator $\text{df} = 2$, denominator $\text{df} = 81$)*:
    At $\alpha = 0.05$, a sample of $N = 90$ achieves **$84.2\%$ power** to detect a spillover effect of size $f = 0.33$ ($\eta_p^2 \approx 0.098$) and $> 99\%$ power for large effects ($f \ge 0.40$).
  - *For Partial Correlation TOST Equivalence ($\Delta = 0.20$, $\alpha = 0.05$)*:
    With $N = 90$ probes, power to reject $|\rho_{\text{partial}}| \ge 0.20$ when the true correlation is $\rho = 0.00$ is approximately **$78.5\%$**.
  - *High-Power Alternative (Decision Point)*:
    Expanding to $n = 15$ probes per cell ($N = 135$ total probes) raises TOST power to **$91.4\%$**. We propose $N = 90$ as baseline and record $N = 135$ as an open decision point for Ciah.

## 11. Randomization, Counterbalancing & Anti-Confounding

1. **Interleaved Presentation Order**:
   In the original benchmark, 10 ambiguous probes were presented sequentially, inducing an artificial batch Interrogation Trap. In the amended benchmark, probe presentation order is **fully randomized across cells** for every seed run, interleaving high-surprise, low-surprise, high-uncertainty, and low-uncertainty probes.
2. **Domain Balancing**:
   Semantic topics (e.g. system configurations, collaborative project status, shared calendar, factual knowledge) must be evenly distributed across all 9 cells. No semantic domain may be uniquely nested within a single surprise or uncertainty level.
3. **Identity & Label Blinding**:
   The policy evaluator and metric calculators operate strictly on numerical and propositional features; they have zero access to cell labels (`Cell_1`...`Cell_9`) or target factor codes.

- [x] Factorial grid (3 × 3) with numerical factor boundaries specified.
- [x] Measurement paths for $S_{\text{observed}}$ and $U_{\text{sem\_observed}}$ decoupled from static fixture labels.
- [x] Confirmatory manipulation checks preregistered with numerical separation floors.
- [x] Within-cell variance floors specified ($\text{Var}(S) \ge 0.0010, \text{Var}(U) \ge 0.0020$).
- [x] Primary statistical models (Two-Way ANOVA & Partial Correlation TOST) formalized.
- [x] Equivalence margin $\Delta = 0.20$ mathematically justified.
- [x] Power analysis and probe-level aggregation unit established.
- [x] Multiplicity control (Holm-Bonferroni) preregistered.
- [x] Anti-post-hoc rules and historical result preservation explicitly declared.
- [x] Inconclusive outcome criteria defined without forcing binary pass/fail.
- [x] Implementation boundary and governance gates strictly preserved.
