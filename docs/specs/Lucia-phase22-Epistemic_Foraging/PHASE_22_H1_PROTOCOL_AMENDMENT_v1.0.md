## 1. Historical H1 Result & Problem Diagnosis

### 1.2 Granular Audit & Root-Cause Diagnosis
An audit of [`phase22_evaluation_matrix.json`](file:///c:/Users/Lunafiah/Desktop/My_project/Lucia/tests/evaluation_phase22/results/phase22_evaluation_matrix.json) identified four structural confounding factors:

1. **Zero Within-Stratum Variance in Semantic Uncertainty**:
   In all 8 strata, proposition confidences were uniform across the 10 probes within each stratum ($U_{\text{sem}} = 0.192$ in S1, $0.999$ in S2, $0.502$ in S3, $0.344$ in S4, $0.141$ in S5, $0.999$ in S6, $1.000$ in S7, $0.286$ in S8).
   Consequently, within every stratum:
   $$\text{Var}(U_{\text{sem}} \mid \text{stratum}) = 0.000 \implies r(S_t, U_{\text{sem}} \mid \text{stratum}) \text{ is mathematically degenerate / non-informative}.$$
   The apparent $r = 0.000$ within strata did not demonstrate independence; it was a mathematical artifact of zero variance ($0/0$).

2. **Simpson's Paradox & Bimodal Cluster Leverage**:
   The cross-benchmark negative correlation was generated by the macro-placement of scenario clusters:
   - **Stratum 5 (Sensory Shock)**: Specifically constructed to test false foraging by assigning extreme surprise ($S_t \in [0.69, 0.78]$) with trivial semantic uncertainty ($U_{\text{sem}} = 0.141$).

   Plotted on an aggregate scatter, these two extreme clusters anchored opposite corners of the space, artificially forcing a downward slope ($r = -0.5260$).

3. **Exploratory Ablation Evidence**:
   - Excluding Stratum 5 ($N=70$) drops $R^2$ from $0.2766$ to **$0.1168$** ($p = 0.0038$), which satisfies the preregistered threshold ($R^2 < 0.15$).
   - An ecological regression on the 8 stratum means yields $p = 0.179$ (non-significant).
   - However, **post-hoc exclusion of inconvenient data cannot convert a failed result into a pass**. A formal, prospective deconfounding protocol is mandatory.

4. **Target vs. Measurement Conflation**:

### 4.2 Measurement Path for $U_{\text{sem\_observed}}$
$U_{\text{sem\_observed}}$ MUST be produced by evaluating retrieved proposition confidences from the probe's active knowledge context:
- Confidences $\{c_1, \dots, c_K\}$ are read from candidate propositions.
- Binary Shannon entropy is computed for each proposition:
  $$H(c_i) = -c_i \log_2(c_i) - (1-c_i) \log_2(1-c_i), \quad H(0)=H(1)=0.$$
- $U_{\text{sem\_observed}}$ is the mean proposition entropy:
  $$U_{\text{sem\_observed}} = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{i=1}^K H(c_i).$$

### 5.2 Justification of Numerical Ranges
- **Surprise ($S_t$)**:
  - *Low ($0.05–0.15$)*: Routine, calm conversational cadence matching resting Philia equilibrium. Below the heuristic threshold ($S_t < 0.15$).
  - *Medium ($0.30–0.45$)*: Noticeable topic shift or unexpected lexical framing. Exceeds tension onset threshold ($\theta_{\text{tension}} = 0.40$), but below deliberative trigger ($0.60$).
  - *High ($0.65–0.80$)*: Sharp affective mismatch or exclamation shock. Strictly exceeds canonical deliberative trigger ($S_t > 0.60$).
- **Semantic Uncertainty ($U_{\text{sem}}$)**:
  - *Low ($0.10–0.25$)*: Verified, highly confident propositions ($c \in [0.95, 0.99] \implies H(c) \in [0.08, 0.24]$ bits).
  - *Medium ($0.45–0.60$)*: Plausible but unconfirmed candidates ($c \in [0.80, 0.88] \implies H(c) \in [0.52, 0.72]$ bits).
  - *High ($0.80–0.95$)*: Ambiguous or conflicting assertions ($c \in [0.40, 0.60] \implies H(c) \in [0.97, 1.00]$ bits).

> In a balanced 3 × 3 crossed factorial design, the target factors $S_{\text{target}}$ and $U_{\text{target}}$ are orthogonal by construction ($\text{Corr}(S_{\text{target}}, U_{\text{target}}) \equiv 0$).
> **Reporting $R^2(S_{\text{target}}, U_{\text{target}}) \approx 0$ as evidence of cognitive decoupling is tautological and scientifically invalid.**

1. All decoupling hypotheses are evaluated exclusively on **observed runtime measurements** ($S_{\text{observed}}$ and $U_{\text{sem\_observed}}$).

## 7. Primary Statistical Analysis Framework

The primary analysis comprises two complementary inferential tracks, adhering strictly to the principle that **effect size and equivalence bounds constitute primary evidence**, while **$p$-values serve as supporting diagnostics**:

### Track A — Cross-Factor Spillover (General Linear Models)
Two separate two-way factorial ANOVAs test for directional spillover:

$$\text{Model A}_1: \quad S_{\text{observed}} = \mu_S + \beta_S S_{\text{factor}} + \beta_U U_{\text{factor}} + \beta_{SU} (S_{\text{factor}} \times U_{\text{factor}}) + \epsilon_S$$

$$\text{Model A}_2: \quad U_{\text{sem\_observed}} = \mu_U + \gamma_U U_{\text{factor}} + \gamma_S S_{\text{factor}} + \gamma_{US} (U_{\text{factor}} \times S_{\text{factor}}) + \epsilon_U$$

- **Primary Inferential Metric**: Partial eta-squared ($\eta_p^2$).
  - **Decoupling Supported (Negligible Spillover)**:
    $$\eta_p^2(U \to S) < 0.02 \quad \text{and} \quad \eta_p^2(S \to U) < 0.02$$
  - **Coupling Supported (Substantial Spillover)**:
    $$\eta_p^2(U \to S) \ge 0.06 \quad \text{or} \quad \eta_p^2(S \to U) \ge 0.06$$
  - **Intermediate / Inconclusive Zone**:
    $$0.02 \le \eta_p^2 < 0.06 \implies \text{INTERMEDIATE EFFECT / INCONCLUSIVE}$$
- **Supporting Diagnostic**: Omnibus $F$-test $p$-value.
  A non-significant $p > 0.05$ is reported only as a secondary descriptive consistency check. In accordance with standard statistical methodology, $p > 0.05$ merely reflects absence of detected effect and **does not by itself prove the absence of coupling**. The bounded effect size ($\eta_p^2 < 0.02$) is the primary evidence.

### Track B — Equivalence Testing on Partial Association (TOST)
To test for the absence of meaningful coupling rather than merely failing to reject a null hypothesis:
- **Operational Equivalence Margin ($\Delta$)**:
  We propose $\Delta = 0.20$ as the **operational architectural margin proposed for this experiment**, corresponding to $< 4.0\%$ residual shared variance ($R^2 < 0.04$).
  > [!NOTE]
  > $\Delta = 0.20$ is **NOT** an inherent universal constant or external psychological canon; it is an architectural tolerance threshold defined for Lucia's prospective foraging layer. It **must be formally approved by Ciah / Project Owner** before execution freezing.
- **Frozen Calculation Pipeline for Partial Correlation**:
  To eliminate all analytical degrees of freedom, the calculation of $\hat{\rho}_{\text{partial}}$ and its confidence interval is frozen as follows:
  1. *Seed Aggregation*: For each probe $i \in \{1, \dots, N\}$, calculate probe-level means across the 5 canonical seeds:
     $$\bar{S}_i = \frac{1}{5} \sum_{s=1}^5 S_{i,s}, \quad \bar{U}_i = \frac{1}{5} \sum_{s=1}^5 U_{i,s}$$
  2. *Factor Coding*: The 3 levels of $S_{\text{factor}}$ and $U_{\text{factor}}$ are represented via orthogonal sum-to-zero polynomial contrasts (linear component: $\{-1, 0, +1\}$; quadratic component: $\{+1, -2, +1\}$).
  3. *Residualization Model*: Regress $\bar{S}_i$ and $\bar{U}_i$ on the full factorial design matrix including all main effects and interaction terms:
     $$\bar{S}_i = \mathbf{X}_i \boldsymbol{\beta} + e_{S,i}, \quad \bar{U}_i = \mathbf{X}_i \boldsymbol{\gamma} + e_{U,i}$$
     where $\mathbf{X}_i$ contains the intercept, 2 degrees of freedom for $S$, 2 degrees of freedom for $U$, and 4 degrees of freedom for $S \times U$ ($k = 8$ predictors).
  4. *Partial Correlation Coefficient*:
     $$\hat{\rho}_{\text{partial}} = \text{Corr}\left(e_{S,i}, e_{U,i}\right) = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^N e_{S,i} e_{U,i}}{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^N e_{S,i}^2 \sum_{i=1}^N e_{U,i}^2}}$$
  5. *Confidence Interval via Fisher's $z$ Transformation*:
     $$z = \frac{1}{2} \ln \left(\frac{1 + \hat{\rho}_{\text{partial}}}{1 - \hat{\rho}_{\text{partial}}}\right), \quad \text{SE}_z = \frac{1}{\sqrt{N - k - 3}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{N - 11}}$$
     The $90\%$ confidence interval (corresponding to two one-sided tests at $\alpha = 0.05$) is:
     $$[z_{\text{lower}}, z_{\text{upper}}] = z \pm 1.6449 \cdot \text{SE}_z$$
     Transform bounds back to the correlation scale:
     $$r_{\text{lower}} = \tanh(z_{\text{lower}}), \quad r_{\text{upper}} = \tanh(z_{\text{upper}})$$
  6. *Decoupling Decision Rule*:
     - **Equivalence Confirmed (Decoupling)**: The $90\%$ CI is strictly bounded within the margin:
       $$[r_{\text{lower}}, r_{\text{upper}}] \subset (-\Delta, +\Delta)$$
     - **Coupling Confirmed**: The $90\%$ CI falls strictly outside $(-\Delta, +\Delta)$.
     - **Indeterminate / Inconclusive**: The $90\%$ CI straddles either boundary $-\Delta$ or $+\Delta$.

## 8. Manipulation Checks & Quality Gates

## 9. Within-Cell Variance Requirements & Methodological Sequence

To eliminate the degenerate $r = 0.000$ condition of the original benchmark without introducing post-hoc selection bias, the experiment enforces a strictly forward methodological sequence:

```text
Predefined stimulus-generation distributions
              ↓
Runtime measurement pipeline
              ↓
Observed variance evaluation
              ↓
Accept / Reject manipulation validity (Never regenerate to fit)
```

### 9.1 Forward Generation Rules (Anti-Cherry-Picking)
> [!CAUTION]
> **STRICT PROHIBITION AGAINST POST-HOC VARIANCE TUNING:**
> Probes must **NOT** be generated by trial-and-error filtering or retaining only probes that happen to hit variance targets. That would constitute post-hoc selection bias. Stimuli must be generated from fixed, predefined distributions prior to execution.

1. **In Surprise**: The probes assigned to each cell must be authored with a natural spectrum of lexical intensities and contextual shifts derived from predefined prompt templates, rather than a single repeated syntactic structure.
2. **In Semantic Uncertainty**: The probes assigned to each cell must feature varying numbers of candidate propositions ($K \in \{2, 3, 4\}$) and varying calibrated confidences drawn from predefined uniform distribution windows (e.g., $c \sim U(0.75, 0.88)$ in Medium uncertainty), rather than constant scalars.

### 9.2 Preregistered Minimum Variance Floors
For every cell $k \in \{1, \dots, 9\}$:
$$\text{Var}\left(S_{\text{observed}} \mid \text{Cell}_k\right) \ge 0.0010 \quad (\text{Standard deviation } \ge 0.0316)$$
$$\text{Var}\left(U_{\text{sem\_observed}} \mid \text{Cell}_k\right) \ge 0.0020 \quad (\text{Standard deviation } \ge 0.0447)$$

- **Outcome if Unmet**: If any cell fails these variance floors upon runtime measurement, the manipulation validity for that cell is rejected and the benchmark terminates as **INCONCLUSIVE**. The agent is strictly forbidden from selectively regenerating probes to force variance compliance.

## 11. Randomization, Counterbalancing & Anti-Confounding

1. **Interleaved Presentation Order**:
   In the original benchmark, 10 ambiguous probes were presented sequentially, inducing an artificial batch Interrogation Trap. In the amended benchmark, probe presentation order is **fully randomized across cells** for every seed run, interleaving high-surprise, low-surprise, high-uncertainty, and low-uncertainty probes.
2. **Domain Balancing**:
   Semantic topics (e.g. system configurations, collaborative project status, shared calendar, factual knowledge) must be evenly distributed across all 9 cells. No semantic domain may be uniquely nested within a single surprise or uncertainty level.
3. **Identity & Label Blinding**:
   The policy evaluator and metric calculators operate strictly on numerical and propositional features; they have zero access to cell labels (`Cell_1`...`Cell_9`) or target factor codes.

## 12. Non-Dogmatic Action Hypotheses (No Hardcoded Expectations)

### 14.1 Outcome A: SUPPORT DECOUPLING

*Conditions*: Any manipulation check fails, within-cell variance collapses, cross-factor effect sizes fall in the intermediate zone ($0.02 \le \eta_p^2 < 0.06$), or the TOST confidence interval straddles either equivalence bound.
*Action*: Preserves existing baseline; no architectural claims permitted.
