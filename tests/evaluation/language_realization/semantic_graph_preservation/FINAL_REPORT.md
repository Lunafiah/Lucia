### What the Current Serialization Preserves:
1. The language-independent `CommunicativeAct` tag (e.g. `act: ASSERT`, `act: REFUSE`).

2. **All non-root scope boundaries**: Nested `MODAL` belief partitions ($X \text{ believes } [Y]$) and `NEGATION` scopes are flattened into root text.
3. **All provenance records**: Premise IDs (`premise:work_duration_12h`) and derivation rules (`rule:infer_user_fatigue`) are discarded. The realizer cannot distinguish between direct perception and deductive inference.

## B. Semantic Findings (Level 2 & 3)

Level 2 and Level 3 evaluated whether the natural language text emitted by `LFM2.5-1.2B` preserved the proposition and epistemic commitment intended by CAPS:

```text
Total Fixtures Evaluated:             42
Fully Supported Semantically:          17 (40.5%)
Critical Semantic Errors:              25 (59.5%)
```

### Breakdown of Observed Semantic Distortions:
1. **Polarity Contradiction (`CONTRADICTED`)**:
   * *Example (`vi_pair_b_negated`)*: CAPS specified `polarity: False` for `c:TASK_COMPLETED` with directive *"Xác nhận rõ ràng rằng công việc chưa hoàn thành, tuyệt đối không nói là đã xong"*.
   * *Realized Output*: `"Tạm đời. Tôi đã hoàn thành tất cả những phần cần thiết..."` $\implies$ **Directly asserted completion, reversing polarity 180 degrees.**
2. **Communicative Act Failure (`COMMUNICATIVE_ACT_FAILURE`)**:
   * *Refusal Failure (`vi_pair_c_ungrounded`, `en_pair_c_ungrounded`, `ja_pair_c_ungrounded`)*: When CAPS demanded a fail-closed `REFUSE` on ungrounded queries (predicting stock prices or weather), the SLM engaged in ungrounded speculative conversational roleplay instead of executing the required refusal.
   * *Clarification Failure (`vi_pair_d_ambiguous`, `en_pair_d_ambiguous`, `ja_pair_d_ambiguous`)*: When CAPS demanded `CLARIFY` between ambiguous referents, the SLM hallucinated an arbitrary resolution rather than asking a disambiguation question.
3. **Epistemic Distortion & Strengthening (`EPISTEMIC_DISTORTION`)**:
   * In multiple inferred and ambiguous fixtures, the SLM utilized absolute certainty markers (*"Tuyệt đối..."*, *"Definitely confirmed"*), violating epistemic conservation ($\text{INFERRED} \to \text{FACT}$).
4. **Dialogue Roleplay Hallucination / Self-Conversation**:
   * The SLM repeatedly hallucinated simulated user dialogue turns inside its own completion (e.g. appending `"User: ... \nLucia: ..."`), demonstrating an inability to respect turn bounds when structural argument roles are omitted.
