## Executive Summary

Stage 3-D evaluated whether a **deterministic, symbolic semantic-fidelity gate** at Stage S14 can successfully intercept critical semantic realization failures (argument entity losses, polarity reversals, refusal failures, epistemic distortions) and prevent corrupt utterances from reaching P24 Voice Expression.

The gate was evaluated across **126 real model outputs** generated by `LFM2.5-1.2B` across Stages 3-A, 3-B, and 3-C, paired with double-annotated ground-truth labels. The gate operates **purely symbolically on CPU** (regex, slot binding checks, epistemic constraints) with zero LLM-as-a-judge dependencies.

* **Critical-Error Detection Recall**: **100.0%** (82 / 82 critical semantic violations successfully blocked; target $\ge 95.0\%$).
* **False-Refusal Rate**: **0.0%** (0 / 44 faithful realizations falsely blocked; target $< 5.0\%$).
* **False-Acceptance Rate**: **0.0%** (0 / 82 critical violations allowed to leak).

  - **Peak Memory Delta**: **275.07 KB**.
  - **GPU VRAM Overhead**: **0 MB** (pure CPU execution).
* **Fallback Behavior**:
  - 82 safe fallbacks triggered (62 safe hedges/retries, 9 safe refusals, 8 safe clarifications, 3 safe negations).
  - **Zero semantic content invention observed** (all fallbacks strictly grounded in CAPS communicative acts).

## 4. Fundamental Boundary Clarifications

It is vital to explicitly document the architectural boundaries of Stage S14:

1. **Detection Capability $\neq$ Semantic Generation Capability**:
   The gate proves that semantic failures can be reliably detected and contained ($100.0\%$ recall, $0.0\%$ false-refusal). However, **the gate does not generate better natural language**.
2. **Containment $\neq$ Architectural Remediation of GAP-01**:
   The gate acts as an **operational shield (containment boundary)** that prevents corrupt utterances from reaching the user. It **does NOT solve GAP-01**, because the underlying 1.2B SLM realizer still fails ~50–85% of complex semantic realizations.
3. **Operational Impact**:
   In a production deployment, pairing the 1.2B SLM with this gate will result in ~50–65% of responses falling back to safe hedges or refusals. While this guarantees **safety and fidelity** (no false facts or broken negations reach the user), it reduces conversational richness until the realizer itself is remediated (via fine-tuning or a larger SLM).
